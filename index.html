Marine Science, Pollution & Environmental Risk

I am a marine scientist investigating how oil pollution affects marine organisms and ecosystems, and how scientific modelling can support environmental protection and evidence-based decision-making.

My work connects marine ecology, environmental toxicology, particle-tracking models, and spatial risk assessment. I am particularly interested in understanding the consequences of chronic oil pollution and the potential impacts of offshore hydrocarbon exploration.
