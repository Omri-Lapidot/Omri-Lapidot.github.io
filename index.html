About Me

Science for healthier marine ecosystems

I am a marine scientist and PhD researcher investigating the environmental consequences of oil pollution in marine ecosystems. My work focuses on understanding how oil spills spread, which marine organisms may be exposed, and how environmental modeling can help assess potential ecological impacts.

My research integrates oil spill trajectory modeling, species sensitivity assessment, and spatial analysis to connect pollution scenarios with vulnerable ecological and coastal assets.

Alongside academic research, I have experience in applied marine biology and underwater imaging technology, including work connecting biological expertise with machine learning development.

Research Interests

Understanding pollution and ecological risk

My research combines biological knowledge and environmental modeling to investigate marine pollution and its potential consequences for ecosystems and coastal resources.

🌊

Oil Spill Trajectory Modeling

Simulating oil transport and dispersion under different oceanographic and weather conditions to investigate potential exposure patterns.

🐟

Marine Species Sensitivity

Assessing the potential sensitivity of marine organisms to petroleum hydrocarbons and exploring approaches to predict effects in understudied species.

📍

Environmental Risk Assessment

Integrating pollution scenarios with spatial information about sensitive habitats and coastal infrastructure to support environmental planning.
